Documentation

Write a formula.
Watch it transform.

The Manim Formula Editor makes formula animations, and it runs in your browser. Write formulas in LaTeX, place them on a timeline, relate the glyphs of one formula to the glyphs of the next, and the editor animates the change. Export the scene as Python for ManimCE or ManimGL, or as a video.

These pages teach it from your first formula to the last detail. You need no Manim to start: the editor plays the animation on its own.

01 / AT ONE LOOK

What the editor does

Four steps make an animation, and each has a panel of its own in the editor.

  1. Write

    1

    Write each formula in LaTeX in the Creator, in a visual math field or in its raw LaTeX, which stay in step.

  2. Place

    2

    Drag formulas onto the tracks of the Timeline, where each clip says when it shows, and place them in the Preview.

  3. Transform

    3

    Relate glyphs from one formula to the next (one to one, one to many, many to one), or morph glyphs within one formula for a derivation.

  4. Export

    4

    Export the scene as Python for ManimCE or ManimGL, as a video, or its sound alone.

Two optional parts. Chroma Lab, the colour studio, comes inside the app. The companion, a small Python helper you run on your computer, adds exact LaTeX glyphs, font text, fast video encoding with ffmpeg and rendering with Manim. You need neither to start.
