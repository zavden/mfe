Widgets

Small interactive pieces that make an idea click: the reader plays with the idea itself, outside the app, on the app's own glyphs and scene engine. Each one is live here with its options and the markup that embeds it.

This page is for the authors of the documentation. widgets/README.md says how to embed a widget, every option, and how to make a new one.

Embedding a widget

A widget is a div.mfe-widget whose data-mfe-widget names it and whose data-options holds its options as JSON, in single quotes. The page loads the widget's script, its stylesheet, the player (the widgets draw with its engine) and the data of the examples the widget names:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="../widgets/widgets.css">
<script src="../assets/player.js" defer></script>
<script src="../examples/widget-link.data.js" defer></script>
<script src="../widgets/link-glyphs.js" defer></script>
…
<div class="mfe-widget" data-mfe-widget="link-glyphs"
     data-options='{"example": "widget-link"}'></div>

When the head lacks the player, the example's data or the stylesheet, the widget loads them itself, a moment later: list them in the head all the same, so that the widget shows at once. Any example of examples/ can be given to a widget, not only the widget-… ones made for this page.

Linking glyphs: link-glyphs

The relation editor's drawing: the source formula on top, the target below. Click a glyph of the source, then one of the target, to link them. Shift, Ctrl or ⌘ adds glyphs to the selection of a side, and Link makes one pair of them: several glyphs onto one, or one onto several. Delete link (or the Delete key) removes the pairs of the selected glyphs; Suggest links by label proposes the pairs the app suggests. Preview plays the transformation with your pairs; the glyphs nobody pairs leave with the source's outro and arrive with the target's intro.

The same relation with no pairs to start from:

OptionValueWhat it does
examplea slugThe example (required).
relationan id, "from:to" or a numberThe relation: its id, the names of its two formulas, or its place in the file (1 for the first). Two clips can have several relations: the names then give the first of them, so use the id or the place. Default: the first.
start"app", "empty"Start from the example's own pairs (default), or from none.

The window of a relation: relation-window

The two clips on a small timeline, the relation's window on both, and the room the clips leave it (dashed). Drag the window to move it in time, an edge to make it longer or shorter; Shift leaves the 0.05 s grid. The window stops where the app stops it. The scene above plays with that timing.

OptionValueWhat it does
examplea slugThe example (required).
relationan id, "from:to" or a numberThe relation. Default: the first.

Rate functions: rate-functions

Every rate function the app offers, as a curve of progress over time with a dot on it, and a glyph that moves that far: the same seconds, played out differently. A click on a curve shows that moment. The switch shows ManimCE's curves or ManimGL's.

A few of them, in ManimGL, with a glyph of an example:

OptionValueWhat it does
functionsa list of namesThe rate functions shown, in that order. Default: every one the app offers.
target"ce", "gl"The library whose curves it starts with (default ManimCE); the reader can switch.
durationsecondsHow long a run lasts (default 2).
example, formulaa slug; an id or nameThe glyph that moves: the first glyph of that formula (default: an italic x).

The ends of a line: line-anchors

The compass of a line's end: the nine points of the box of the glyphs it is on, and Auto, the edge Manim finds towards the other end. Click a dot on the scene, or a key of a compass, to put that end there: the arrow is placed and built again at once.

OptionValueWhat it does
examplea slugThe example (required).
formulaan id or a nameThe line. Default: the first line whose ends are on glyphs.
timesecondsThe instant shown. Default: once the line is drawn.

Styles side by side: style-compare

One relation played with each style the app offers for a pair, on one clock.

A pair of several glyphs onto one, with each way its sources can arrive:

OptionValueWhat it does
examplea slugThe example (required).
relationan id, "from:to" or a numberThe relation. Default: the first.
stylesa list of "transform", "copy", "fade", "fade_copy"The panels, in that order. Default: all four.
paira numberOnly this pair takes each style (1 for the first); the others keep theirs. Default: the whole relation.
extrasa list of "separate", "merge", "copies"Compares how the sources of pair (several glyphs onto one) arrive, instead of styles.

Palettes by role: palette-roles

A scene made with each palette the app comes with: the editor's own colours and the themes of Chroma Lab. Hover a glyph to read its role, or a role of the legend to light its glyphs; with the scene focused, the arrow keys go from glyph to glyph.

OptionValueWhat it does
examplea slugThe example (required).
palettesa list of idsThe palettes offered: editor, midnight-chalkboard, ink-and-paper, orbital-violet, light-laboratory. Default: all.
roles{"formula": {"role": [indices]}}Roles of glyphs by hand, by formula id or name, over the ones read from the example's colours.
timesecondsThe instant shown. Default: the end of the scene.

A widget that cannot show

An option the widget cannot read, an example that has no data or a relation it does not have: a notice in its place says what is wrong, and the rest of the page goes on.
